About Sneha

Sneha is a landscape and portrait artist specializing in acrylics. Her work explores light, texture, and color through layered brushwork and thoughtful composition.

Artist Statement

Working primarily with acrylics, Sneha seeks to capture fleeting moods of landscapes and the quiet personality of sitters. Commissions and prints are available — contact for details.
