About Sneha

Sneha is an acrylic painter working with layered color and textured brushwork to capture quiet moments in landscapes and the nuance of portraiture. She accepts commissions and offers limited edition prints.

Artist Statement

I work intuitively with acrylics, building layers of color and texture until a quiet atmosphere emerges. My landscapes focus on light and mood; my portraits aim to reveal personality through simplified forms and thoughtful color choices. Each piece is hand-painted and available for commission.

Commissions & Prints

If you'd like a commissioned portrait or landscape, please get in touch. Limited edition prints are available on request.
